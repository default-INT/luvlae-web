# DTC bundles — Stage 2 catalog and stock services

Local implementation only; no remote migration, catalog provisioning or provider calls. Stage 1 migration `008-dtc-bundles` must precede new writers. Pack checkout wiring and administration forms are later stages.

- New products initialize `offers: []` and `inventory.version: 1`. Catalog PATCH uses allowlisted `$set` and increments only catalog `version`; any legacy `stockQuantity` PATCH is rejected with `INVENTORY_ADJUSTMENT_REQUIRED`.
- Offer mutation endpoints: `POST products/:sku/offers`, `PATCH products/:sku/offers/:offerId`, `POST products/:sku/offers/:offerId/duplicate`; these are under `/api/admin`. Creation/duplication generate UUIDs, return draft offers, and persist result IDs in transactional audit for replay. Active offers cannot become draft; archived offers are immutable and may be duplicated. Limits are 20 nonarchived/100 total records.
- `PATCH /api/admin/products/:sku/storefront` accepts `homeOfferIds`, `highlightedOfferId`, `highlightBadge`, catalog `version`, `operationId`, `reason`. At most three unique standard/active refs; archiving removes home/highlight references atomically.
- `POST /api/admin/products/:sku/inventory/adjustments` accepts `{ mode: 'delta' | 'set', quantity, expectedInventoryVersion, operationId, reason }`. `quantity` is the signed delta or target stock. It checks safe nonnegative stock >= reserved, records audit and increments inventory version only. Reservation, mock, Stripe commit/release, manual restock and refund restock writers likewise increment inventory version without catalog changes.
- Public API and entity server projection retain `id` as product ID, base `price`, and `availableQuantity` in boxes; add `purchaseOffers` with `offerId`, `unitsPerPack`, `patchesPerPack`, `price`, `availableQuantity` in packs, and `sortOrder`. Standard is virtual, followed by active offers sorted by order/ID. Admin `offers` remains the full records including statuses/timestamps.
- [`offer-calculator.server.ts`](../apps/web/src/_app/commerce/offer-calculator.server.ts) resolves only published offers and optionally verifies catalog version. Calculator aggregates physical demand across offers, limits each SKU to 99 boxes, rejects duplicate lines, and checks safe cents/provider upper bound. Legacy checkout contracts remain unchanged until Stage 3.

Verification: focused service tests cover lifecycle/replay/archive cleanup, shared stock demand, stock version/replay/concurrent adjustment and route auth/Origin/no-store. Full server suite at the review freeze passed 149/151; only the two recorded baseline automatic-tax/email-trigger assertions failed. Independent review remains separate.
