Only the two video paths use runWorkerFirst. `_app/cloudflare/video-assets.ts` fetches ASSETS and supplies full/HEAD/206/416 responses with correct MIME and byte ranges. Requests to these paths invoke the Worker and incur the account's Worker request usage. Other assets retain their existing routing.
